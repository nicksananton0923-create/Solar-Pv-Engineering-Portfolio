| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | The framework shall provide CSV templates for module and inverter registers. | IMPLEMENTED |

| FR-03 | The framework shall provide illustrative calculations for string configuration. | IMPLEMENTED |
